Researching component adoption

Mapped why teams weren’t using shared components and proposed a rollout plan with measurable success criteria.

My role
UX Research, facilitation, stakeholder alignment
Methods
Stakeholder interviews · diary prompts · synthesis
Deliverables
Adoption barriers map · rollout plan · measurement plan

Problem

Teams rebuilt similar UI patterns repeatedly, but adoption of shared components was low and inconsistent.

Key barriers

  • Discoverability: people didn’t know which components existed or when to use them.
  • Trust: uncertainty about accessibility, responsiveness, and edge cases.
  • Workflow friction: unclear contribution process and long feedback loops.

What we changed

  • Clarified “when to use” guidance and examples.
  • Introduced a lightweight intake process and a changelog cadence.
  • Defined adoption metrics (usage, duplication rate, satisfaction).
