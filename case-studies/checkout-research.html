Reducing checkout drop-off

A focused study to identify top friction points in checkout and validate fixes with usability testing.

My role
UX Research (lead), partner with product & design
Methods
Interviews · funnel review · moderated usability tests
Deliverables
Findings readout · prioritized issues · recommendations

Problem

Checkout abandonment was high, and the team needed clarity on which issues to fix first and why.

Approach

  • Reviewed funnel metrics and support tickets to form hypotheses.
  • Interviewed users to understand motivation, expectations, and trust concerns.
  • Ran usability tests on the existing flow and prototype fixes.

Key findings

  • Users hesitated when fees and delivery details were revealed late.
  • Address and payment fields lacked guidance, causing errors and rework.
  • Trust signals were inconsistent across steps, increasing uncertainty.

Impact

Implemented fixes were tracked with a lightweight measurement plan (completion rate, error rate, and step drop-off).
