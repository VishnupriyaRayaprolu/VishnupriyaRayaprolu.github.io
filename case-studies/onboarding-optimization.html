Improving onboarding clarity

Evaluated an onboarding flow, pinpointed comprehension gaps, and shipped copy + structure improvements.

My role
UX Research, synthesis + recommendations
Methods
Task-based testing · quick survey · thematic analysis
Deliverables
Annotated flow · content guidelines · test results

Problem

New users were uncertain what to do next, leading to incomplete setup and early churn.

What I did

  • Defined success criteria with product (completion + comprehension).
  • Ran task scenarios to observe where users paused, misinterpreted, or dropped.
  • Converted issues into concrete copy and IA recommendations.

Recommendations

  • Reordered steps to match user mental models.
  • Added plain-language explanations and examples at decision points.
  • Introduced progress feedback and clearer next actions.
